Paid media can put a manufacturer in front of a query this month. It can also buy clicks from students, competitors, and people who needed a different machine. Demand generation is the discipline of separating those clicks from enquiries sales can quote, then connecting the spend to a page and a reply.

@@body

A click is not a project

Industrial purchases move through engineering, procurement, and sometimes a distributor. The person who searches today may be checking a dimension, not signing a purchase order. Paid search still has a job: appear for a defined product query while a page is new, while a shutdown creates short-term demand, or while you test whether a family has search demand at all. The landing page has to match the query. A campaign for a pressure class that opens on the homepage wastes the click.

Search engine marketing, in practice, is that match across Google and, where the account data supports it, Microsoft. The keyword list should come from the catalog and from sales, not from a generic industry list. Queries that name a supplier need and a product you can quote deserve a tight ad group. Research queries belong on an application page, or they should be left alone until the page exists.

Paid social is a different kind of demand

LinkedIn can reach named accounts and job roles when you already know which plants or OEMs you want. It is a poor place to explain a tolerance. Facebook and Instagram sometimes fit brands with a visual product and a dealer network. They are a weak default for a component that is specified from a drawing. Account-based programs and remarketing only help when you have a real list or a real prior visit, and when the next message adds a specification or a case the buyer can check.

Outbound and email sit next to this, not under it. A cold sequence that asks for a meeting before it states the product will be ignored. Nurturing is justified when sales has agreed to follow a specific action, such as a quote request that includes a model number. A newsletter signup is not that action unless someone actually calls it back.

What to count

Report enquiries that name a product or an application, and keep poor-fit clicks visible so the account can be cut back. Quotes issued, on the sales team's calendar, matter more than the cheapest cost per click. Do not divide revenue by ad spend and call it a proven return. The cycle is longer than the click, and more than one page is usually involved.

How paid demand stays tied to sales capacity

Search ads without a landing page produce expensive visits. Social campaigns without a defined account list produce awareness you cannot trace to a quote. Remarketing without a useful next page only repeats the same slogan. Email and outbound fail when they are measured as volume instead of as conversations sales will take.

Start from the families the plant can quote this month. Add a paid channel only for those queries, and only with a person assigned to the technical reply. Broader social and account programs can wait until that path is staffed.
