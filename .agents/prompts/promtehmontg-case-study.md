# Cursor Prompt: Update PromTehMontg Case Study

Use this file together with `.agents/prompts/case-study-generation.md` when creating
or updating the PromTehMontg Case Study. Do not apply these requirements to other
facade clients.

The published source paragraph describes general SEO and website work for a
ventilated-facade installer. Use that as background. Do not copy generic facade
benefits verbatim. The public case must show the specific digital-marketing work
for a company that designs, prepares/processes, and installs facade systems across
several cladding materials.

Update the existing PromTehMontg Case Study in Payload CMS. Do not create a
duplicate case, new collection, or new route. First inspect the current Case Study
record, schema, available content blocks, media relationships, and existing
frontend rendering.

## 1. Case positioning

Present PromTehMontg as a facade engineering and installation company providing
integrated solutions for ventilated facades, including design, preparation and
processing of materials, mounting-system engineering, supply of fastening
components, and installation.

The case should demonstrate work in website structure, SEO strategy, keyword and
competitor research, semantic-core development, content planning, and optimization
of service and material pages.

Do not describe the project as merely a general website redesign or claim that
specific business results were achieved unless they are documented in the existing
record.

## 2. Facade materials and solutions

Integrate the following actual product and service categories into the case
content where relevant:

- Aluminum composite panels.
- Ceramic-magnesite facade boards.
- Aluminum facade cassettes.
- Natural stone cladding.
- Galvanized steel facade cassettes.
- Brick-effect facade panels.
- Facade substructures and mounting systems designed for these different cladding
  materials.
- Engineering, preparation, processing, and installation of facade systems.

Explain the relationship between the cladding materials and the supporting
structures. Emphasize that different materials require appropriate mounting
solutions and engineering considerations.

Use technically accurate language. Do not invent product specifications,
certifications, test results, project measurements, or claims about energy
efficiency that are not supported by the available information.

Preserve the material names as supplied. Do not silently replace ceramic-magnesite
boards with a different material, and do not assume that brick-effect panels are
made from actual brick.

## 3. SEO and website architecture

Describe the work in terms of:

- Search intent and competitor research.
- Semantic-core development and keyword clustering.
- A logical website structure organized around services, facade materials,
  mounting systems, and engineering solutions.
- Unique, search-oriented content for relevant service and material categories.
- Clear navigation that helps construction companies, developers, contractors,
  architects, and other professional buyers find relevant solutions.

Do not invent ranking improvements, traffic growth, lead volumes, conversion
rates, or financial results. Do not invent page URLs or claim that every material
category had its own landing page unless the source confirms it.

The published source names core website sections for services, materials, and
technical expertise, plus custom design aligned with professional branding. Treat
those as verified. A keyword count, CMS name, and duration are not published.
